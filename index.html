About Us

Genesis Landscaping was founded by Colton Walker in 2017 in Courtice, Ontario. Our current headquarters is located in Oshawa, and we service customers fairly throughout Durham, York, & Clarington Regions. Family-run with a focus on honesty and integrity, our #1 mission is leaving every customer with fond memories of their experience with us. With humble beginnings in simple lawn maintenance, our services rapidly expanded to offer landscaping, hardscaping, fencing, and snow removal – both commercial and residential.
